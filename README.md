# DataSeriesTableau
This project is an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of a Superstore sales dataset, visualized using Tableau. The dashboard provides key insights into sales performance across various dimensions, including regional sales, product subcategories, order quantity distribution, profit trends, and future sales forecasting.

The data used in this analysis was obtained from Kaggle : https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final

## Goals:
- Analyze sales and profit distribution across regions and product categories.
- Identify key product subcategories contributing to revenue.
- Understand order quantity trends across customer segments.
- Explore the relationship between sales and profit for different product types.
- Provide forecasting insights for future sales performance.

## Key Insight:
1. Sales by Region:
- The West region has the highest sales at 725K, followed by the East (679K) and Central (501K) regions.
- The South region has the lowest sales at 392K, suggesting potential for market expansion.
2. Sales & Profit Relationship by Product Category:
- Technology products have a higher sales volume but show profit fluctuations.
- Furniture products tend to have lower profits despite decent sales.
3. Order Quantity Distribution by Segment:
- The Consumer segment has the highest order volume (52.96%), followed by Corporate (30.12%) and Home Office (16.92%).
- Businesses may consider targeted marketing towards high-order segments.
4. Sales by Subcategory:
- Phones (330K), Binders (203K), and Accessories (167K) contribute the most to revenue.
- Lower-performing subcategories like Paper (78K) may need pricing or marketing adjustments.
5. Forecasting Sales & Profit Trends:
- Sales and profit show an upward trend, with expected growth in future quarters.
- Q4 consistently performs better than other quarters, indicating seasonal demand spikes.

## Advice & Recommendation
1. Expand market presence in the South region to boost sales.
2. Optimize pricing and cost structure for furniture to improve profitability.
3. Leverage high-demand products (Phones, Accessories) for promotions and upselling.
4. Prepare for Q4 demand surges by stocking up on high-performing products.
5. Further analyze product return rates to improve customer satisfaction and retention.
